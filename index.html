TECHNICAL PROBLEM STATEMENTS

The 4 Innovation Challenge Tracks

Engineered around real national infrastructure challenges and enterprise cloud scale, mentored directly by SRM KTR professors and Microsoft architects.

T1 Microsoft Azure Track

High-Resilience Enterprise Cloud Systems

Design and implement fault-tolerant, horizontally scalable multi-tenant services capable of zero-downtime failover across Azure paired regions using Cosmos DB multi-master replication and AKS.

Evaluation: Chaos engineering test resilience, latency under 5,000 concurrent RPS, and automated IaC (Terraform / Bicep).
Supported Stack: Azure Functions, Cosmos DB, AKS, GitHub Actions.
T2 SRM KTR Research Hub

Applied Edge Intelligence & Edge Analytics

Build low-power on-device machine learning inference engines for sensor networks, smart agriculture, or acoustic fault detection without relying on persistent cloud connectivity.

Evaluation: Quantization benchmarks (ONNX Runtime, INT8), battery-draw profile, and inference latency under 15ms.
Supported Stack: ONNX, Embedded C++, TinyML, Raspberry Pi / Jetson emulation.
T3 Delhi University Mission

Urban Mobility & Civic Air Quality Intelligence

Leverage real-time Delhi NCR environmental sensors and public transit telemetry (DTC bus & Delhi Metro APIs) to optimize dynamic last-mile commuter routes and hyper-local smog mitigation.

Evaluation: Algorithm efficiency on live Delhi sensor datasets, geospatial spatial-temporal modeling, and citizen UX.
Supported Stack: PostGIS, OpenData Delhi APIs, React, Python geospatial.
T4 Open Innovation

Zero-Trust Security & Open Banking Infrastructure

Create provably secure decentralized identity protocols, verifiable credential systems (India Stack / DigiLocker integration), or robust fraud detection systems for high-volume transactions.

Evaluation: Cryptographic soundness, OWASP Top 10 hardening, penetration testing durability.
Supported Stack: Rust, Go, OpenID Connect, OAuth2.0, WebAuthn.

CLARIFICATIONS & POLICIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participation, logistics, and rules.

Is there any registration or participation fee?

+
No. Admission is 100% free. Thanks to full sponsorship by SRM Institute of Science & Technology (SRM KTR) and Microsoft India, participation, high-speed Wi-Fi, Azure credits, merchandise, and all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, midnight snacks) are fully covered for all shortlisted teams.

Who is eligible to participate in the hackathon?

+
Undergraduate and Postgraduate students enrolled in any recognized engineering, computer science, or technology institution across India are eligible. Inter-college and inter-year teams (2 to 4 members) are allowed. Cross-institutional collaboration between SRM KTR and Delhi University students is warmly encouraged!

What is the exact start and end schedule?

+
The event commences on Monday, 19 October 2026 at 11:00 AM IST with delegate credentialing and inaugural keynotes. It runs continuously through Day 2 (20 October), and formally concludes on Wednesday, 21 October 2026 at 3:00 PM IST following the Valedictory Awards ceremony.

How do outstation accommodations work?

+
Teams selected from outside the Delhi National Capital Region (including visiting delegates traveling from SRM Kattankulathur campus) will receive complimentary university dormitory accommodation from the evening of October 18 to the evening of October 21. Check the "Accommodation Required" checkbox during registration.

What hardware and software must we bring?

+
Teams should bring their own laptops, chargers, extension cords, and any specific IoT or hardware kits required for their prototype. High-speed Wi-Fi, Ethernet patch cables, 230V power points at every desk, and Microsoft Azure cloud access keys will be provided.
